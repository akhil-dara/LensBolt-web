# LensBolt privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy covers the LensBolt app for Android. In short, LensBolt collects nothing. It has no access to the
internet, and your files stay on your phone.

## What the app collects

Nothing. LensBolt has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It contains no
code that sends data to us or to anyone else.

## No network access

The app does not ask for Android's network (internet) permission, so it cannot connect to the internet or to any
server. Files reach the phone only through the camera, from a screen that you point it at.

## The camera

LensBolt asks for camera access for one purpose: to read the pattern that the LensBolt sender shows on a screen.
The app processes camera images in memory on your phone and then discards them. It does not record or save them,
and it never sends them anywhere. (A test mode for developers can keep a few camera frames in the app's private
storage. It can only be turned on from a computer with Android developer tools, and a normal start of the app
deletes those frames.)

## Your files

- A file you receive is kept in the app's private storage on your phone until you save it, share it or start a
  new transfer.
- **Save** uses Android's own file picker, and the file goes to the place you choose. **Open** and **Share** pass
  the file to the app you pick, and only when you ask.
- The app keeps a short history on the phone: the names, sizes and dates of files you saved, and where you saved
  them. This history and the app's settings stay in the app's private storage.
- The app's data is left out of Android backups and device-to-device transfers. Uninstalling LensBolt deletes
  its private storage, including the history. Files you have already saved elsewhere stay where you put them.

## Links that open your browser

The app may show links to pages outside the app, such as the project's page or a donation page. Tapping one opens
your web browser, and LensBolt itself sends nothing. The page you open is run by its own operator, and that
operator's privacy policy applies.

## Google Play

If you install LensBolt from Google Play, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy, for
example to provide the developer with crash and performance statistics. LensBolt adds nothing to that.

## Children

LensBolt is a general file-transfer tool. It is not aimed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date at the top.
