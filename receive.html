Open LensBolt on the computer that has the files and press Start there. Then choose how this device reads its screen.

  • Nothing leaves this deviceNo network, no upload, no account. The picture stays on the device.
  • Only verified filesEach file is offered once its SHA-256 matches the sender's.

How does this device read the sender?

How it works
  1. Phone camera. On the phone, open this page (on iPhone: Receive in Safari) or the LensBolt app on Android, and choose Phone camera. Hold the phone so the square on the computer's screen fills most of the picture; the guide says when it reads.
  2. Webcam, PC to PC. On the computer that receives, open this page and choose Webcam. Turn its camera, or the other laptop, so the sender's square fills most of the picture.
  3. Share a window, one computer. When a window on this computer shows the sender (a VM, a remote desktop, or another computer's screen shared to this one), choose Share a window and pick that window. Nothing to aim or hold; choose Fastest on the sender.

Each file is offered once its SHA-256 matches the sender's. Nothing is sent back to the sender.
